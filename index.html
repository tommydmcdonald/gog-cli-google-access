Personal Google Workspace integration

Google Workspace,
from a personal computer.

This private integration uses gog-cli to work with the owner's Google account through commands and an AI assistant.

What it does

With the account owner's permission, it can read and manage Gmail, calendars, Drive files, contacts, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and tasks. Actions depend on the permissions granted and the commands the owner runs.

Who it is for

This is a personal integration for the owner and people they know. It is not a public sign-up service. Access to a Google account requires that account owner's authorization.

Privacy and control

Read the privacy policy for details about Google account access, local storage, and AI providers. You can revoke access through your Google Account connections.

This integration uses open-source gog-cli and is not affiliated with Google. For questions, contact the application owner who provided you access.
