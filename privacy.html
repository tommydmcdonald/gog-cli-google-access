Privacy policy

This policy covers the owner's personal gog-cli integration with Google Workspace and this informational website.

Google data accessed and used

The integration uses Google OAuth to request access to the Google services the account owner authorizes. Depending on the granted permissions and requested task, this may include email messages and settings, calendar events, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets, contacts, and tasks.

Google data is accessed to carry out the owner's commands, such as finding information, drafting or sending email, updating a calendar, or working with files. The integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and AI providers

gog-cli runs on the owner's computer. OAuth refresh tokens are stored in the computer's operating-system keychain in this setup. Command output and exported files may be stored locally, and information used in an assistant session may appear in that session's history or logs.

When the owner uses an AI assistant to work with Google data, information needed for the requested task may be sent to the configured AI provider and any tools the owner chooses to use. Those services process information under their own privacy policies and account settings. Running gog-cli locally does not mean that every assistant interaction stays on the computer.

Retention and revocation

Local credentials, files, and session records remain until the owner removes them or the relevant service applies its retention settings. The account owner can revoke this integration's access at any time through Google Account connections. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously saved files or assistant session records; those must be managed in the places where they were stored.

This website

This site is informational. It has no sign-up form, tracking scripts, or advertising. The hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site. Google account credentials and account data are not submitted through this website.

Contact and changes

For questions or requests about this personal integration, contact the application owner who provided you access. Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
